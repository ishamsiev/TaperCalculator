# Taper Calculator

Everyday machinist geometry, right inside ENCY. A compact, ENCY-styled window
that opens from the utilities menu and **stays open next to your work** while you
keep using ENCY — no project or geometry is ever touched.

Type a few known values, press **Solve**, and read off the rest. Both `.` and `,`
work as the decimal mark; angles are in degrees; diameters and lengths share
whatever unit you type.

## What it solves

### Taper / Cone
Enter any **three** of {big diameter *D*, small diameter *d*, length *L*, half
angle *α*} and it finds the fourth — plus the **included angle** and the
**taper ratio 1 : X**. Perfect for cones, tapered shanks and transitions.

### Right triangle
Enter two sides, or one side and angle *A*, to get every remaining side and
angle. Handy for **chamfers, slopes and angular allowances**.

### Metric thread (ISO)
Enter the nominal diameter and pitch — or leave the pitch empty to use the
**standard coarse pitch** — to get the fundamental height *H*, pitch diameter
*d₂*, minor diameter *d₁*, thread depth *h₃* and an approximate **tap-drill
diameter**.

## How to use

1. Open the utilities menu in ENCY and click **Taper Calculator**.
2. Pick a tab and fill in the known values.
3. Press **Solve** to compute, or **Clear** to reset the tab for the next part.

## Notes

- **Non-modal** — the window lives alongside your work and can stay open.
- **Self-contained** — it does the math locally and modifies nothing in your
  project or geometry.
- Dark, ENCY-matched UI, including the title bar.
